// our story

We're on a mission
to make compliance
human.

Founded in 2018 by a team of CAs, lawyers, and engineers tired of watching great businesses struggle with needless bureaucracy.

From frustration
to a platform

Our founders spent years watching businesses lose momentum to compliance confusion — missed deadlines, wrong filings, surprise penalties. The paperwork was getting in the way of the real work.

So in 2018, we built CorpEase — a platform that combines expert professionals with smart workflows to make compliance genuinely effortless. We started with just company registration and today cover everything a business needs.

We've since helped over 50,000 businesses across India navigate registrations, tax filings, trademark protection, and annual compliance. Every feature we ship is born from a real problem a client faced.
